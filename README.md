## Project Overview

Brass & Beyond is an e-commerce brand offering jewelry, body care products, and wellness essentials.

This project involved transforming an existing Shopify storefront into a cohesive, visually engaging shopping experience inspired by 1970s retro aesthetics, Afrocentric influences, tropical florals, and warm earthy tones.

The goal was to improve the store's visual identity, product organization, navigation, and overall customer shopping experience while keeping the Shopify platform manageable for the business owner.

## The Challenge

The original storefront relied heavily on Shopify's default presentation, with limited branding, minimal navigation, and products displayed in a basic grid.

The redesign focused on creating a stronger brand identity while making the storefront easier for customers to explore.

## My Contributions

### Branding & Visual Design
- Developed a cohesive retro-inspired storefront aesthetic.
- Incorporated a custom floral brand logo and coordinated imagery.
- Established a warm cream, chocolate brown, burnt orange, and pink color palette.

### Storefront Customization

### Product Organization
- Organized products into customer-friendly collections.
- Improved product naming and descriptions.
- Enhanced product discovery through clearer navigation and collection pages.

### Customer Experience
- Improved desktop and mobile presentation.
- Customized product page and cart styling.
- Applied coordinated branding to the Shopify checkout.
- Organized customer-facing informational pages and store policies.

## Design Direction

The visual direction combines:

- 1970s-inspired retro aesthetics
- Afrocentric and cultural influences
- Tropical botanical imagery
- Warm earth-tone colors
- Expressive typography
- Jewelry, beauty, and wellness storytelling

The finished storefront reflects the brand's message:

**Adorn Yourself. Inside & Out.**
